YumeHashi was made for exactly that.

## Three Steps to Turn Dreams into Action

### Step 1. Write it down

Just put your dream into words. Once written, your brain starts treating it as a "goal."

### Step 2. Break it down

Turn the **dream** into **goals**, and goals into **tasks**. The big wall becomes a daily series of small actions.

```
Dream: become an IT engineer
  └ Goal: get an AWS certification (within 3 months)
      └ Task: work through the textbook one chapter at a time
      └ Task: take practice exams
```

### Step 3. Keep going

Your accumulated activity lights up as a **constellation**. Completing the 12 constellations becomes the proof of your growth.

## Main Features

| Feature | Description |
|---|---|
| Dream / goal / task management | Hierarchical organization with discovery guides |
| Schedule | Visualize tasks on a timeline. Reminders and CSV export supported |
| Book management | Manage learning books with a bookshelf-style UI, including reading schedule and reviews |
| Statistics & analysis | Visualize activity time, streaks, and per-goal progress |
| Constellation system | Each piece of activity lights a star; the 12 constellations form a gamified journey |
| Dashboard | Customizable view of today's status, streaks, and personal bests |
