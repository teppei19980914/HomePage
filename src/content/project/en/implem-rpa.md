## Project Overview

Project to automate the customer-renewal work that was a sales-side bottleneck (40 hours per month spent), so the team could redirect effort toward core sales work.

**Project size**: 3 members

## Responsibilities

- As PM, authored requirements, specifications, and design documents
- Directed and managed two junior members (task / schedule / motivation / conflict management)
- Drove cross-team improvement together with the sales department and the development department
- Managed progress and quality from a QCD perspective

## Results

| Achievement | Detail |
|---|---|
| 40 person-hours / month saved | Full RPA of the contract-renewal process eliminated the manual workload entirely |
| Zero error rate | Designed the system to prevent human errors (e.g., wrong recipients) by structure, achieving a zero-error rate |
